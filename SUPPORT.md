# Support

## What these repositories are

Productside publishes digital takeaways and examples that demonstrate and extend our teaching and advisory services: classes, workshops, webinars, consulting, advisory engagements, and lead generation.

They are provided as is, as instructional material. **They are not a software product, and this is not a product support channel.** Nothing published here carries a support obligation, a service level commitment, or a maintenance commitment.

That said, we would rather hear from you than not. Here is what to expect.

## What to expect, honestly

| You do this | We do this |
|---|---|
| Open an issue or pull request | We review open items **weekly**. We do not promise a turnaround time, and we may close items we are not going to act on |
| Report a security concern | We acknowledge within **3 business days**. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not open a public issue |
| Ask about training, consulting, or partnership | Use the [Productside website](https://productside.com/contact/). GitHub is the slowest way to reach us commercially |
| Ask us to fix or customize material for you | That is consulting. Please [get in touch properly](https://productside.com/contact/) |

We would rather tell you we review weekly and keep to it than promise 24 hours and miss it.

## Where to ask

- **GitHub issues** — factual problems: a broken link, a typo, an instruction that does not work, a missing file
- **GitHub discussions**, where enabled — broader questions about how to use something
- **[productside.com/contact](https://productside.com/contact/)** — anything commercial: training, consulting, advisory, partnership, or licensing
- **`github@productside.com`** — if email is genuinely easier for you

## If you are a Productside client or a course participant

**Do not use GitHub.** Your engagement has a named contact and your agreement sets out how support works. Reach out through your normal channel and you will get a faster and better answer.

Please also do not post client-specific questions, materials, or data in a public issue. These repositories are world-readable.

## Good requests include

- what you were trying to do
- what happened instead
- the exact wording of any error or unexpected output
- which file or resource you were using

Please do not include real customer, learner, or participant data in an issue, even as an example. Use made-up data.
